## Project rules

- Opening-scene geometry lives in `src/components/deepotsav/ghat.ts` in source-photo pixels; diyas, ghat light and tests all derive from it, so re-measure there if the photo changes (never hand-place lamps).
- Fireworks are layered behind `temple-foreground.webp` (real alpha cut-out of the lit photo); keep that layer order so bursts never cover the temple or names.
- The build is always a static prerender into `dist/client`; set the `PAGES_BASE` env var (e.g. `/repo-name/`) in `vite.config.ts` builds for a GitHub Pages sub-path, default `/`.
- Sections below the opening live in `src/components/wedding/` and react to the opening only from outside (`opening-signals.ts` watches its START/REPLAY buttons); why: the opening in `src/components/deepotsav/` is self-contained and must not depend on page sections.
- All guest-facing text goes through `wedding/i18n.tsx` (EN + HI dictionaries with identical keys) and wedding facts through `wedding/details.ts` with IST offsets; why: one place to change copy, dates and the Hindi switch.
- Recolour opening photos (`temple-ghat-*.jpg`, `temple-foreground.webp`) only with pixel-exact grading that keeps size and alignment, never AI image edits; why: diya and ghat-light positions are measured on those exact pixels, and the cut-out's RGB must stay identical to the lit photo.
- Opening layout lives in `deepotsav/layout.ts` (`computeScene`): the photo is always flush with the screen's bottom-right corner and keeps the whole temple in frame; wide screens extend the river by mirror-tiling the photo's left strip, tall phones show a second closer window of only the ghat stone (feathered outline mask) standing in the temple's river; why: one source of truth for every screen size, covered by the screen-fit tests.
- `font-display` resolves through the `--face-display` variable: the opening keeps its own face, and `.invite-type` (invitation sections + top bar) overrides it; why: invitation-page type can change without touching the self-contained opening.
- The invitation velvet background is one pre-rendered seamless tile (`src/assets/velvet-tile.webp`, wrap-around noise) on `.paper`; why: SVG feTurbulence background layers render with hard seams where tiles restart.
- Background music is one shared looping `<audio>` player in `wedding/music.ts` (track is a bundled asset in `src/assets`); every play button (top bar, floating orb) and the opening's START go through it; why: one source of truth for play state and fades. The "Our song" card links out to the couple's favourite song on Spotify, separate from the background track.
- The browser scrollbar is hidden; `wedding/ScrollAids.tsx` draws the diya scroll rail on the right and the floating "Next Page" link, which walks `PAGE_IDS` in page order; why: keep new sections in that list so the link reaches them.
